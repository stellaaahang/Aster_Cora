# Aster / Cora
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
delegated: The user explicitly authorized selecting a modern stack for this empty project. React, TypeScript and Vite provide a portable, lightweight local website.
## Users
International innovation competition judges, potential research partners and space technology visitors.
## Product Purpose
Explain Cora, its ground prototype, proposed in-cabin missions, modular design and the Aster student team.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Four pages, full English/Simplified Chinese resources, English on first visit, persistent explicit language choice. Responsive and keyboard accessible interactions. Use the supplied product imagery and the original Cora pixel art in the companion demo. Present modules and station tasks at their verified development status.
## Brand Commitments
User-pinned Orbital Minimalism, supplied reference image, Aster logo and actual Cora renders. White and ice blue, spacious layouts. Code-first delivery explicitly requested: a runnable project, not mockups.
## Evidence on Hand
User-supplied local DOCX, technical PDFs, product renders, scene illustrations, module concepts and team photo. CAD archive contains SolidWorks SLDPRT/SLDASM files rather than a web-ready GLB/GLTF. Team names and roles are provided in the bilingual innovation brief; no individual portrait-to-name mapping is assumed.
## Product Principles
Visual clarity, product accuracy, usable interactions, consistent branding and performance, in that priority order.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, touch controls, reduced motion, responsive English and Chinese layouts.
